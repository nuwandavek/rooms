# Home brief

Describe the home, who the study is for, and the current scope (shell, materials,
furniture, or a lighting study). Record the source plan and photo provenance.

The initial model is a fictional 6 × 4 metre room, not a measured home.
Replace it using source evidence before presenting it as a reconstruction.

## Visual direction

Riso: cream paper, navy outlines, blue controls, pink and yellow accents.
Pale materials and soft daylight in the model.
Lighting is illustrative; it is not a geolocated sun or illuminance simulation.
